# Offscroll Times Legal Compliance Review

Reviewed: 22 September 2026. This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice or counsel approval.

## India

- The privacy notice and account controls cover notice, purpose limitation, access, correction, deletion, grievance contact and retention concepts under the Digital Personal Data Protection framework. Final notices, consent language, breach procedure and the identity of the Data Fiduciary must be reviewed against the DPDP Act and the rules in force at launch.
- The checkout and policies disclose product characteristics, seller-contact placeholders, price components, delivery, cancellation and refund handling for the Consumer Protection Act and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules. The legal entity, grievance officer, GST treatment, invoice particulars and complaint timelines remain launch blockers.
- Official review sources: [MeitY DPDP materials](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-) and [Department of Consumer Affairs e-commerce rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules_0.pdf).

## European Union and United Kingdom

- The privacy notice identifies data categories, purposes, recipients, retention and data-subject controls. Counsel must determine controller identity, lawful bases, processor terms, international-transfer mechanism, representative/DPO obligations and each launch country's required wording.
- The cookie interface blocks optional first-party analytics until affirmative choice and provides an accessible withdrawal/reset control. Reassess if any storage, embedded media or advertising technology is added.
- Checkout exposes characteristics, duration, quantity, item price, discounts, delivery, tax, total, manual renewal and cancellation links before payment. The cancellation policy recognises that an eligible EU distance-sale withdrawal period is generally 14 days and that newspaper/periodical exceptions may differ for subscriptions.
- VAT/IOSS, importer-of-record status, customs handling and destination-specific invoicing must be decided before enabling each European country. Do not promise duty-paid delivery until the operating model is confirmed.
- Official review sources: [European Commission Consumer Rights Directive](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/consumer-contract-law/consumer-rights-directive_en), [EU pre-contract information guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/contract-information/index_en.htm), [EU VAT e-commerce/IOSS](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/index_en), and [ICO storage and access technology guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/).

## Mandatory launch blockers

- Insert the verified legal entity name, registered and trading address, registration identifiers, GST/VAT identifiers where applicable, phone number and named Indian grievance officer.
- Confirm the countries actually served, trader/importer-of-record model, VAT/GST calculation, delivery terms and returns address.
- Obtain written review and approval from qualified Indian counsel and counsel covering every enabled European/UK market.
- Record approval owner, counsel, scope, version of every policy reviewed and approval date. Re-review after material product, provider, country, pricing or legal changes.
