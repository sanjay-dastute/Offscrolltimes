# Contact enquiry delivery

WhatsApp: +91 73730 50093.

Validated contact enquiries are stored in D1 for administrator access, then
forwarded through FormSubmit to hello@offscrolltimes.com. Turnstile, the honeypot
and existing enquiry rate limits run before forwarding. Private staff notes
are never forwarded. No newsletter or marketing subscription is created.

Before launch, submit one real enquiry and check hello@offscrolltimes.com
(including spam) for FormSubmit's activation message. Complete any required
activation and submit a second enquiry to verify inbox delivery and Reply-To.
The integration has not been verified against an activated live inbox.

An email_sent value means FormSubmit accepted the request, not that the email
was delivered. If forwarding fails, the enquiry remains available in Admin.
There is no automatic retry; review enquiry records for missed notifications.

Contact form data (name, reply email, country, enquiry detail and message) is
shared with FormSubmit for forwarding. Review this processor in the approved
privacy policy before production.

Provider documentation: https://formsubmit.co/ajax-documentation
