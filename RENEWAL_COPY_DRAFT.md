## Subscription and Renewal Terms

A subscription is a prepaid purchase for the selected number of monthly editions.
The subscription will automatically renew for the same selected duration unless
cancelled before the renewal date.

### Term and entitlement

Choose 1, 3 or 12 months and a quantity. Total entitlement is the months multiplied
by monthly quantity. Start date, paid-through date and expected first edition are
recorded with the order.

### Automatic renewal

At the end of the selected subscription term, the subscription will automatically
renew for the same duration and quantity using the payment method authorised for
the subscription. The applicable price at the time of renewal will be charged.

An in-account reminder will appear near the end of the current term. You may cancel
before the renewal date to prevent the next automatic renewal.

### Cut-off and cancellation

Orders and address changes received by the displayed cut-off normally apply to the
next eligible edition.

Cancellation of a subscription does not automatically erase paid entitlement or
create a refund. Mandatory withdrawal and refund rights remain available.

### Changes

Later price changes never alter a completed order. If the renewal price differs
from the price paid for the previous term, the applicable renewal price will be
shown before renewal. Material service changes affecting a paid term will be
explained before they take effect.
